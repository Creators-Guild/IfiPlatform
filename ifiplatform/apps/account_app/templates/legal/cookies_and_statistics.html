{% trans "Cookies and statistics" %}

{% trans "Cookies" %}

{% blocktrans %}IfiPlatform does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and does not sell your data or share it with anyone for their own use. The only cookies in use are Django's own session and CSRF cookies, required for login and form security.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Visitor statistics" %}

{% blocktrans %}To see which pages are used, we count visits and clicks on some links with Umami, which runs on IfiPlatform's own server. Umami uses no cookies, doesn't store your IP address, and isn't linked to your account. It records the page, the site you came from, and your browser, operating system, device type, language and location (country, region and city). These are general statistics about the site, not about you.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Monthly totals" %}

{% blocktrans %}We count, per month, things like how many accounts are created, how many pages are published, and how many issues and pull requests are made in the project's public GitHub repository. These numbers and the visitor statistics above are used to follow how the site is used and grows, and in a master's thesis about how people start contributing to the platform. They are totals for the whole site only: they never say who did what, and numbers from 1 to 4 are shown as "fewer than 5".{% endblocktrans %}
